What repair services does the garage provide?

Services include oil and filter changes, engine repairs, gearbox repairs, A/C servicing, painting, denting, electrical and other mechanical work. Contact the team to check the exact repair scope for your vehicle.

Is an online appointment automatically confirmed?

No. Submitting the form stores a request for review. The garage must contact you to confirm your appointment date and time.

Do you offer a 100% service guarantee?

The garage describes its service as guaranteed. Ask the team what guarantees or warranties apply to your particular repair and keep the agreed details with your invoice.

Can you provide an estimate before I visit?

Yes, you can request an initial estimate. Accurate final pricing may require a vehicle inspection, parts selection and confirmation by the garage.
